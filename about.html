About us

A small studio with a big point of view.

We believe great work starts with understanding.

Founded in 2018, Nexus started as a two-person operation with a simple belief: digital products should feel human. Today we're a team of 12, working with startups and established brands across the globe.

We embed ourselves in your problem space, ask the uncomfortable questions, and then build something that actually solves them — beautifully.
